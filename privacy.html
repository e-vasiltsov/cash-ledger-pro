Cash Ledger Pro Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Cash Ledger Pro ("the App") is designed to help you count cash, coins, and register drawers. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

Cash Ledger Pro does not collect personal information or require you to create an account.

The App does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information, precise location information, contacts, photos, or other personal information.

2. Cash Counts and App Settings

Cash amounts, counts, custom denominations, preferences, and other information you enter into the App are stored locally on your device.

This information is not transmitted to our servers because Cash Ledger Pro does not operate a server-based account or cloud storage service for this information.

3. Internet Access

Cash Ledger Pro is designed to work offline. An internet connection is not required to count cash or use the App's core features.

4. Data Sharing and Selling

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Analytics and Tracking

Cash Ledger Pro does not use third-party advertising trackers or analytics services to collect information about your use of the App.

6. Children's Privacy

Cash Ledger Pro does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

7. Data Security

Because the information you enter into Cash Ledger Pro is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers, we do not maintain a remote database containing your cash counts or settings.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device and its operating system.

8. Data Deletion

Since Cash Ledger Pro does not maintain user accounts or store your information on our servers, there is no server-side account or personal data that we need to delete on your behalf.

Information stored locally by the App can generally be removed by deleting the App from your device, subject to how your device manages application data and backups.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or applicable requirements. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the privacy practices of Cash Ledger Pro, please contact us through the support contact information provided on the App Store listing.
