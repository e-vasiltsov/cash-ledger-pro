Cash Ledger Pro Support

Welcome to Cash Ledger Pro support.

Cash Ledger Pro helps you quickly count cash, coins, and register drawers using a simple and offline-friendly interface.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Cash Ledger Pro require an internet connection?

No. The core features of Cash Ledger Pro work without an internet connection.

Where are my cash counts stored?

Your cash counts and app settings are stored locally on your device.

Can I use different currencies?

Yes. Cash Ledger Pro supports multiple currencies and allows you to configure denominations to match your needs.

Can I add custom denominations?

Yes. You can add, remove, or edit denominations according to your currency or register setup.
